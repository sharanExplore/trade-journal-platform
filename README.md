# 📈 TradeVault

### Trading & Investment Journal

**TradeVault** is a personal trading and investment journal designed to help users **record, track, analyze, and improve their financial decisions**.

Instead of keeping trades and investments scattered across spreadsheets and notes, TradeVault brings everything into one place — from individual trades and portfolio holdings to performance analytics and trading psychology.

> **Track your decisions. Understand your performance. Invest with discipline.**

---

## 🎯 Why TradeVault?

Making money in the market is only one part of the process.

Understanding **why a trade worked, why an investment failed, how much risk was taken, and what patterns appear over time** is equally important.

TradeVault aims to turn raw trading and investment data into useful insights.

### The core cycle

```text
Trade / Invest
      ↓
    Record
      ↓
    Analyze
      ↓
 Learn from Results
      ↓
   Improve Decisions
```

# 🚀 Features

## 📊 Trading Journal

Record and analyze individual trades with detailed information such as:

* Asset / Symbol
* Buy & Sell price
* Entry & Exit time
* Position size
* Stop Loss
* Take Profit
* Risk-to-Reward ratio
* Strategy / Setup
* Market condition
* Trade direction
* Profit / Loss
* Trade notes

Track important performance metrics:

* Total Trades
* Winning Trades
* Losing Trades
* Win Rate
* Total P&L
* Average Win
* Average Loss
* Risk-to-Reward
* Profit Factor
* Best Trade
* Worst Trade

# 🧠 Trading Psychology

Numbers don't tell the whole story.

TradeVault also allows users to record the **psychological side of trading**.

Track:

* Emotion before entering
* Emotion during the trade
* Confidence level
* Fear / Greed
* Discipline
* Rule violations
* Reason for entry
* Reason for exit
* Lessons learned

This can help identify behavioral patterns that may not be visible from P&L alone.
